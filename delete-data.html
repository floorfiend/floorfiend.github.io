Delete your data — Floor Fiend

Floor Fiend by Ahquaf Hussain · Last updated: 2 October 2026

Floor Fiend has no accounts of its own and no servers of its own. Your game data lives on your phone and, if you sign in to Google Play Games, in your Google account. Here is how to delete each part.

How to delete your data

  1. Your cloud save (Google Play Games): open Floor Fiend, go to Settings → Delete my cloud save (shown while you are signed in). This deletes your saved progress from your Google Play Games account straight away and turns cloud save off.
  2. The progress saved on your phone: uninstall Floor Fiend, or go to your phone's Settings → Apps → Floor Fiend → Storage → Clear data. This deletes it immediately.
  3. Everything Google Play Games keeps for this game (saved game and achievements): in the Google Play Games app, open your profile → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, and choose Floor Fiend (or delete all your Play Games data).
  4. Or ask us: email ahquaf.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Floor Fiend data". We reply within 30 days. We don't store your data on our own servers, so we'll confirm there is nothing held by us and walk you through steps 1 to 3.
