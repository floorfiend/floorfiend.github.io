Privacy Policy — Floor Fiend

Effective date: 2 October 2026

1. Who we are

Floor Fiend is developed by Ahquaf Hussain (India), an individual developer. Contact: ahquaf.dev@gmail.com

2. Summary

  • No accounts of our own and no servers of our own. We do not see or store your personal data.
  • Your progress is saved on your device and, if you sign in to Google Play Games, in your Google account.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob (Android) and Google AdSense / H5 Games Ads (web), which collect some data as described below. On the web, a consent banner from a Google-certified consent platform appears in the EEA, UK and Switzerland.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's AdMob SDK collects: your device's advertising ID and app set ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, interactions with the app and ads, and diagnostic data. This data is encrypted in transit and processed by Google under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Your choices and consent

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before ads are shown. You can change it any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android device settings.
  • Buying Remove Ads turns off all non-optional ads.

5. Google Play Games Services (sign-in, cloud save, achievements)

If you sign in, Google provides the game with your Play Games player ID and display name, and stores your saved progress and achievements in your Google account. We use this only to sync your progress and achievements. You can manage or delete this data in the Google Play Games app or your Google account. In the game, Settings > "Delete my cloud save" deletes the saved game from your account and turns cloud save off. Google Privacy Policy

5b. Web version: Google sign-in and cloud save (optional)

On the web version you may sign in with Google to sync your progress. We then store your game progress (levels completed, death counts, achievements) in Google Firebase (Firestore), linked to your Firebase user ID, and receive your Google display name to show who is signed in. We do not receive your password. Only you can read or change your save. You can delete it any time with Settings → Delete my cloud save, or sign out to stop syncing. Google Firebase privacy

6. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details; we only receive confirmation that you own Remove Ads.

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Data retention and deletion

Local data is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its storage. Android may keep an automatic backup of game data in your Google account, which you can manage in your device's Google backup settings. Play Games data is managed through your Google account.

9. Security

We store no personal data on our own systems. Data handled by Google is protected under Google's security practices.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR in the EU/UK, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 in India), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data. Since we hold none ourselves, most requests are handled through Google's tools; contact us and we will help.

11. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change and significant changes will be shown in the game.
