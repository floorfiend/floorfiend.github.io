Floor Fiend

The floor is lying. The door is lying. Everything is lying.

Coming soon to Google Play

A platformer that hates you (affectionately)

Run, jump, reach the door. Simple, right? Every level has three stages, every stage has traps that wait for the exact moment you trust them, and one death sends you back to the start of the stage. Your blood stays on the floor so you never forget where you fell.

  • Floors that vanish under your feet
  • Spikes that pop up late
  • Doors that run away
  • Springs you shouldn't trust
  • Ceilings that fall
  • Blocks that appear from nowhere
  • Controls that flip
  • Walls of spikes that chase you

Five worlds of lies

75 hand-made levels across five volcanic worlds, a boss at the end of every road, Google Play Games achievements (plus some very rude in-game ones), and cloud save so your progress follows you to a new phone.
